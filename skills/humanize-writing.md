# Humanize Writing Skill

8-pass editing process:
1. Structure tells (formulaic sections, identical list lengths)
2. Significance inflation / promotional language
3. AI vocabulary and hedging
4. Superficial analysis
5. Vague attributions
6. Robotic rhythm and em dash overuse
7. Chatbot artifacts
8. Human texture and soul (opinions, lived experience, personality)

## Usage
`/humanize-writing` then paste text
