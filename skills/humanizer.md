# Humanizer Skill

Rewrite AI-generated text to sound human-written.

## Based on
Wikipedia "Signs of AI writing" (26 patterns).

## Process
- Detect AI patterns (inflated significance, promotional language, formulaic structure, AI vocabulary, hedging, robotic rhythm, etc.)
- Rewrite preserving meaning
- Second pass: "what still sounds AI?"
- Match requested tone (professional/casual/irreverent)

## Usage
`/humanizer` then paste text, or "humanize this: ..."
