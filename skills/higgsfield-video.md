# Higgsfield Video Skill

Animate images into video and run UGC pipelines.

## Capabilities
- Image-to-video (Kling 3.0, 720p 6s)
- Seedance 2.0 video generation
- Full UGC ad pipeline (character, images, video)
- Playwright browser automation

## Usage
`/higgsfield-video <image or pipeline>`
